Required CSV schema

Column names must match exactly. The waste column is the supervised-learning target.

ColumnTypeMeaning
food_typeCategoryFood category
guestsNumberNumber of guests
event_typeCategoryService or event type
quantity_prepared_kgNumberFood prepared, in kilograms
storage_conditionCategoryStorage quality/condition
purchase_history_kgNumberHistorical amount purchased, in kilograms
seasonCategorySeason
preparation_methodCategoryFood preparation method
locationCategoryGeographical location/category
price_per_kgNumberPrice per kilogram in your dataset currency
waste_kgNumberObserved food waste in kilograms (target)
Numeric features and target must be non-negative. Rows with missing waste targets are excluded; numeric feature values must be present and valid. Additional columns are ignored.
